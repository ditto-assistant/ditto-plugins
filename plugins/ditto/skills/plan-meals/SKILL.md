# Plan meals

Answer "what's for dinner?" for the whole week, for this household, and turn it
into a grocery list.

Use the bundled `ditto-memory` skill for connection, recall and capture rules.

## Learn the household

Search Ditto for who is eating, dietary needs and allergies, likes and dislikes,
cooking skill and equipment, budget, and meals that were hits or misses before.
Allergies and medical diets are hard constraints; confirm them when the record
is old or unclear. Ask for the week's shape: busy nights, guests, leftovers
wanted, and anything already in the fridge to use up.

## The plan

- **Week at a glance:** a meal per slot the user wants planned, quick ones on
  busy nights, with variety in protein and cuisine.
- **Recipes:** short ingredient lists and steps, scaled to the household, with
  substitutions for any restriction. Link a source recipe when you adapt one.
- **Prep:** what to make ahead and when, and how leftovers become another meal.
- **Grocery list:** combined quantities grouped by store section, with pantry
  items marked "check first".

Note nutrition only in general terms and suggest a professional for medical
diets.

## Learn and repeat

After the week, ask what worked, and save the hits, misses and new preferences
to Ditto on the user's yes, so next week's plan improves. Offer a weekly Ditto
workflow that drafts the plan and grocery list on the day the user shops. Order
groceries only when the user asks and a connected tool supports it, confirming
items and total first.
